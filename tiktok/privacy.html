Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ocrypto_tiktok (the "App") handles information.

1. Information the App receives

When the account owner authorizes the App with TikTok Login Kit, TikTok returns an access token, a refresh token, the account's open ID and the granted scopes (user.info.basic, video.upload). The App receives no other personal information and does not access any other TikTok user's data.

2. How it is used

The tokens are used only to upload the creator's own videos to the creator's TikTok inbox and to check the upload status.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a file readable only by the operator. They are not sent to any server other than TikTok's API.

4. Sharing

The App does not sell, share or disclose information to third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until they expire or until authorization is removed. The account owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app settings (app permissions), after which the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

6. Analytics and cookies

The App does not use analytics or cookies.
